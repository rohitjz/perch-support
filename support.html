Perch Support

Common fixes and contact information for Perch on macOS.

Where is Perch after launch?

Perch is a menu-bar app and has no Dock icon. Look for its timer icon in the macOS menu bar.

Why did the timer notification not appear?

Open System Settings, choose Notifications, select Perch, and allow notifications. The timer continues to work when notifications are disabled.

Does Perch need to stay open for reminders?

You can close the menu panel and leave Perch running in the menu bar. Notifications already scheduled with macOS can arrive after you quit Perch, subject to notification permission and Focus settings. Perch's repeating chime and alarm panel need the app running. Snooze is lost when you quit, so keep Perch running until a snoozed alarm rings again.

Why are global shortcuts not responding?

Open Perch Settings to confirm the shortcuts. If another app owns the same shortcut, quit that app or change its shortcut.

Why are notes not syncing?

Confirm that the same Apple ID is signed in to iCloud on each Mac and that iCloud Drive is enabled. Perch falls back to local notes when private CloudKit is unavailable.

How do I remove a note?

Open the note's More menu and choose Delete. Deletions sync through iCloud when cloud sync is active.

Will the 14-day trial charge me?

No. The trial starts only after you confirm the free App Store transaction. It does not renew, and Perch does not charge you automatically when it ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

The clock and its alarms stay free. Timers, Pomodoro, floating displays, and access to notes require a one-time Lifetime Access purchase at the price shown in Perch. Existing notes remain stored and can be opened again when you buy or restore Lifetime Access. Nothing renews or charges automatically.

Does restoring the trial give me another 14 days?

No. Restore Purchase in Settings > Purchase restores the original trial transaction and its expiry date. Use the same App Store account that started it.

How do I restore Lifetime Access?

Open Perch Settings, choose Purchase, and select Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID that acquired Lifetime Access.

Why is a purchase unavailable?

Confirm that the Mac is online and signed in to the App Store, then open Settings > Purchase and choose Refresh App Store. A pending family or purchase approval must finish before Perch unlocks.
