Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

Perch is designed to work without accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Information Perch stores

Perch stores timer settings, appearance preferences, and shortcut-related state on your Mac. Sticky-note text, formatting, checklists, and pasted images are stored locally with SwiftData.

If iCloud is available, note content can synchronize through your private iCloud/CloudKit database. The Perch developer does not operate a server that receives or analyzes your notes.

Information Perch does not collect

Perch does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, browsing history, contacts, location, diagnostics, or usage profiles. Perch does not sell or share personal information.

Clipboard and notifications

Perch reads the clipboard only when you choose Paste inside a sticky note. Perch requests notification permission when needed to alert you that a timer or focus session has completed.

Purchases

Perch uses Apple's StoreKit to offer a free 14-day trial and a one-time Lifetime Access purchase. Perch reads verified transaction information on your device, including the product, purchase date, and entitlement or revocation state, only to decide whether features are available.

Apple processes purchases and associates them with your Apple ID. The Perch developer does not receive your payment card details and does not keep a server copy of your StoreKit transaction history.

Retention and deletion

Delete a note in Perch to remove it from the app's note store. If iCloud sync is active, deletion is synchronized through iCloud. Apple may retain synchronized data and App Store purchase history according to your Apple account settings and Apple's policies.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email rohitjaiswals@outlook.com or visit the Perch support page. Email you send is used only to answer you, and is not added to a mailing list.
